SUBJECT: "The Black Flame" itself, as the hero image of the series website.
A single tall, majestic flame burning on a dark obsidian floor at the bottom of the world. The flame itself is BLACK: its body is pitch-black / deep violet-black, like living obsidian smoke, with sharp, carved, blade-like tongues (not soft fluffy fire). Its edges are rimmed with thin bright molten orange-gold and crimson light, and a few hot gold cracks glow inside it. Around it, a dim purple-crimson halo lights the smoke; the flame casts warm reflections onto the glossy dark floor, where thin golden seams of a tiled stone floor radiate outward from its base, faintly glowing.
Composition (landscape 1536x1024): flame centred horizontally; its base sits at about 82% of the image height and its tallest tongue reaches about 18% from the top. The left and right thirds are mostly dark smoky sky and shadow (negative space for website text). Ember sparks drift upward. Dramatic, iconic, symmetrical-ish but organic. Edges vignette to near-black.
