Dark fantasy autobattler roguelike

Lead your party to the bottom of the world, where the Black Flame burns. Choose from 60 heroes across nine alliances, arm them with relics, and fight down through three Circles of the underworld. After each boss, ancient powers offer you a pact, for better and for worse.

The Studio

Emberfish Games is a two-person independent studio in São Paulo, Brazil, making strategy games with depth and replayability. Its debut, Hadean Tactics, has sold over 100,000 copies worldwide.
