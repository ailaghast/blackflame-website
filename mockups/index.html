Black Flame · website mockups

Three directions for the series home. Each card opens a working page, responsive and with live effects. All three use the same assets: the standalone BLACK FLAME™ wordmark (cut from the Tactics logo), the v2 sigil SVG, press-kit art, and three Codex illustrations.

A · Descent

Cinematic and game-first. Tactics key art fills the hero with the series wordmark over it. A series row with sealed slots, a Chapter I band, the trailer, stats and a screenshot strip.

B · Monolith

Franchise-first and minimal. The Black Flame itself (new Codex art) is the hero, followed by a one-line manifesto, Chapter I, and carved slabs for sealed future chapters.

C · Index

Editorial, with fact-sheet DNA. A split hero with a numbered series index table and a glowing tactics-grid floor, the abyss vista quote, a fact file, gameplay loop and studio panel.
