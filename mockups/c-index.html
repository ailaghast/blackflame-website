Build your party

Choose from 60 heroes across nine alliances, building a different mix every run. Stack one alliance to grow its strength, or bring together heroes who have no business fighting side by side and find out what they can do.

Call the shots

Hire heroes in the shop, then decide who fights, where they stand and who they go after first. Level them up to three stars, arm them with relics, and watch your plan play out on the battlefield.

Three Circles deep

Fight your way down through three Circles of the underworld, past elite battles, merchants and mysterious events. After each boss, ancient powers rise from the dark to offer you a pact. Each one changes the rest of your run, for better and for worse.

  • 60 heroes and 9 alliances
  • 57 relics
  • 21 ancient pacts from 7 patrons
  • 7 bosses
  • A Daily Run, the same for every player
  • Five difficulty levels
  • An endless Abyss past the final boss
  • Solo or online co-op with a friend

The Studio

Emberfish Games is a two-person independent studio in São Paulo, Brazil, making strategy games with depth and replayability. Its debut, Hadean Tactics, has sold over 100,000 copies worldwide.
